## SECTION 1 - What Is Thosjod?

Body: Thosjod is an AI Website Discovery & Lead Engine that identifies, engages, and qualifies every website visitor in real time, then routes sales-ready leads into your CRM - and natively tracks how your brand shows up across AI answer engines (GEO/AEO).

## SECTION 2 - What Is Drift?

Body: According to Drift's own public description, its main use case is "Conversational marketing and meeting booking," with stated capabilities: Website chat, qualification, routing, account-based engagement and calendar booking. (drift.com)

## SECTION 3 - Where They Overlap

Body: Both products are used to engage and qualify website visitors as part of a GTM motion. Both are positioned specifically for website-embedded sales conversations.

## SECTION 4 - Product Approach

Body: Thosjod is built around one continuous visitor record spanning identification, conversation, qualification, and analytics, plus a dedicated AI-visibility layer. Drift's publicly stated approach centers on: Website chat, qualification, routing, account-based engagement and calendar booking.
