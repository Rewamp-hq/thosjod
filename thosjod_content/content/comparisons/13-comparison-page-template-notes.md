# Comparison Page Rules (apply to every page in this folder)

Per the brief's Section 14 instructions, strictly enforced across all comparison pages:

**Never write:**

- "Thosjod is better than X"
- Arbitrary scores or star ratings
- "Winner" badges or checkmark/X grids implying a verdict
- A capability for a competitor that isn't in their public CSV description
- A limitation for a competitor that isn't publicly documented

**Always write:**

- "Thosjod provides..." / "[Competitor] provides..." (parallel, neutral construction)
- "Not identified in the available source material" for any unconfirmed competitor capability
- "When evaluating these products, teams may want to consider..." to prompt reader judgment instead of asserting a conclusion
- A visible link to the competitor's own official URL, so the reader can verify independently

**Every comparison page ends with the same disclaimer block** (insert verbatim before the CTA):

> This comparison is based on publicly available information as of {date}. Competitor capabilities are drawn from their own published descriptions and may have changed. We encourage you to verify current capabilities directly with each vendor.
