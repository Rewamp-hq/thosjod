## SECTION 1 - What Is Thosjod?

Body: Thosjod is an AI Website Discovery & Lead Engine that identifies, engages, and qualifies every website visitor in real time, then routes sales-ready leads into your CRM - with native AI-visibility (GEO/AEO) tracking built into the same engine.

## SECTION 2 - What Is This Category?

Body: Dedicated AI-visibility (also called GEO or AEO) platforms monitor how often and how favourably AI answer engines mention a brand, which sources those answers cite, and what to change so the brand is cited more. Most are built for marketing, SEO and content teams.

## SECTION 3 - Companies in This Comparison

- **Profound** - AI marketing platform for winning in ChatGPT and AI search platforms (tryprofound.com)
- **Otterly.AI** - AI search monitoring across ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Claude, Perplexity, AI Mode, Gemini and Copilot (otterly.ai)
- **Peec AI** - AI search analytics for marketing teams: visibility, position and sentiment (peec.ai)
- **Scrunch** - AI search visibility and optimization, including AI agent traffic analytics (scrunch.com)
- **AthenaHQ** - Command center for AEO and GEO across 11+ AI platforms (athenahq.ai)
- **Ahrefs Brand Radar** - Brand visibility across AI answers, YouTube and Reddit (ahrefs.com/brand-radar)
- **Semrush AI Visibility** - AI visibility tracking within the Semrush SEO platform (semrush.com)

## SECTION 5 - Where These Tools Go Further

Body: Several of these platforms publicly describe AI-visibility capabilities that are not in Thosjod's current feature list - including citation and source analysis, sentiment measurement inside AI answers, and analytics on AI crawler traffic. Teams whose main need is deep AI-search analytics for a content or SEO program should evaluate these tools on their own merits.

## SECTION 6 - Where the Category Generally Stops

Body: Based on the vendors' own websites reviewed for this comparison, these platforms focus on how a brand appears in AI answers and on the content that shapes those answers. None of the pages reviewed describe identifying or engaging the visitors who then arrive on your website, or qualifying and routing them into a CRM - the part of the journey Thosjod is built around, with AI-visibility tracking in the same engine.

## SECTION 7 - Questions to Consider

- Is your main goal measuring AI visibility, or turning the traffic AI sends you into pipeline - or both?
- Which AI engines matter most for your buyers, and does each tool track them today?
- Do you need citation, sentiment or crawler analytics, or is mention and competitor tracking with a site audit enough?
- Who owns this budget - SEO and content, or revenue and demand generation?
