## SECTION 1 - Ideal Customer Profile
H2: ICP template.
Body: Fill in each field for your best customers. These are the criteria every visitor is scored against.
| Field | What to write | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Industries | The sectors your best customers are in | SaaS, logistics |
| Company size | Employee or revenue range | 200-1,000 employees |
| Regions | Where you can sell and support | India, APAC |
| Roles | Who buys and who uses | Head of RevOps, Marketing lead |
| Need | The problem that makes them buy | Qualified pipeline from website traffic |
| Intent signals | Behaviour that shows active evaluation | Visited pricing twice; viewed integrations |
| Disqualifiers | Who you should not spend sales time on | Students, job seekers, competitors |

## SECTION 2 - Sales agent playbook
H2: Agent playbook template.
Body: Write this in plain language in your Sales Agent's playbook field.
1. **Greet** - Greet visitors on pricing and product pages and offer help with what they are looking at.
2. **Answer** - Answer questions only from our knowledge base. If a question isn't covered, offer to connect them with the team.
3. **Qualify** - Naturally find out company size, use case and timeline.
4. **Offer the next step** - If they match our ICP, offer a 20-minute walkthrough and book it on the right rep's calendar.
5. **Hand off** - If they ask for a person, or the conversation turns negative, hand off to the team with the full context.

## SECTION 4 - Routing rules
H2: Routing rules template.
Body: Start with a few rules like these, in the "if, then" form workflows use.
| If | Then |
|---|---|
| ICP score is 85 or higher and company size is 1,000 or more | Route to the enterprise team |
| Visitor is in APAC | Route to the APAC rep |
| Sentiment drops below 0.3 or the visitor asks for a human | Send to the support queue |
| ICP score is below 50 | Nurture with a playbook; no rep assignment |

## SECTION 5 - Follow-up playbooks
H2: Follow-up playbook templates.
- **Pricing visitors who didn't book** - When a company visits pricing twice without booking, email the identified visitor the next morning with an offer of a 20-minute walkthrough.
- **High-ICP anonymous companies** - When a company with an ICP score of 80 or more is identified, notify its account owner with the pages it viewed.
- **Lost-lead re-engagement** - Thirty days after a lead is marked Lost, send a short note sharing what's new.
