## SECTION 1 - Getting started
H2: Get your workspace live.
1. **Connect your CRM** - Choose HubSpot, or Salesforce on Scale and Enterprise plans, so qualified leads sync automatically.
2. **Define your Ideal Customer Profile** - Set the industries, company sizes, regions and signals that make a visitor a good fit. Every visitor is scored against it.
3. **Configure your first agent** - Pick a Sales or Support agent and add your first knowledge source, such as your sitemap or pricing page.
4. **Install the widget** - Add your workspace snippet to your website. No engineering sprint is required.

## SECTION 2 - Billing and credits
H2: How billing works.
- **Plans** - Every plan has a one-time setup fee, a monthly platform fee and a monthly credit allowance: Starter (5,000 credits), Growth (20,000), Scale (75,000) and Enterprise (custom). (`/pricing`)
- **Credits** - 1 credit per AI conversation turn, 2 per visitor identified and enriched, 3 per AI-visibility prompt check per engine, and 50 per full GEO/AEO audit.
- **Overage** - Usage beyond your included credits is billed per credit at your plan's rate, or you can upgrade.
- **Changing plans** - Upgrades apply immediately and are pro-rated; downgrades take effect at the next billing cycle.
- **Usage** - See credits used this cycle, your plan and your next invoice date under Settings, Billing.

## SECTION 3 - Agents and knowledge
H2: Keep answers accurate.
- **Grounded answers** - Agents answer only from the knowledge sources you enable, and hand the conversation to your team instead of guessing.
- **Adding knowledge** - Add pages, PDFs, docs or a sitemap under Knowledge. Re-sync a source after you update it.
- **Knowledge gaps** - Questions your agents keep handing off appear under Knowledge, Gaps, so you know what content to add next.
- **Scope** - Narrowing an agent's scope limits what it can discuss and reduces the risk of wrong answers.
- **Languages** - Up to 10 languages on Growth and 175+ on Scale.

## SECTION 4 - Integrations
H2: Connect your stack.
- **HubSpot** - Two-way sync of contacts, deals and conversation transcripts. (`/integrations/hubspot`)
- **Salesforce** - Lead and opportunity routing on Scale and Enterprise plans. (`/integrations/salesforce`)
- **Calendars** - Google Calendar and Microsoft Outlook / Office 365, so agents can book meetings inside the conversation. (`/integrations/calendar`)
- **Webhooks and API** - Send conversation and lead events to your own systems; API access is on Scale and Enterprise. (`/integrations/api`)

## SECTION 5 - Account and security
H2: Access and permissions.
- **Team** - Invite teammates under Settings, Team, and give each one a role.
- **Roles** - Default roles are Admin, Editor and Viewer; you can create custom roles under Settings, Permissions.
- **Single sign-on** - SSO is available on Scale and Enterprise plans.
- **Audit log** - Every configuration change is recorded under Settings, Audit Log.
- **Passwords** - Reset your password from the login page with "Forgot password?". Thosjod support will never ask for your password.

Q: What happens if I run out of credits?
A: Usage continues and extra credits are billed at your plan's per-credit rate, or you can upgrade to a plan with a larger allowance.

Q: Why did my agent hand a conversation to a human?
A: Agents only answer from your enabled knowledge sources. If none of them covers a question, the agent hands off rather than guessing. Check Knowledge, Gaps to see which questions to cover next.

Q: Can I use Salesforce on the Growth plan?
A: Salesforce is available on Scale and Enterprise plans. Growth includes two CRM integrations plus webhooks.

Q: How do I get API access?
A: API access is included on Scale and Enterprise plans. Generate keys under Settings, API.

Q: Who do I contact for account-access problems?
A: Use the contact page and choose Existing Customer Support.
