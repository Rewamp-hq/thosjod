## SECTION 1 - The problem with forms
H2: Forms only catch the visitors who volunteer.
Body: A contact form only captures the small, self-selected share of visitors willing to hand over their details. Everyone else researches, compares and leaves, and every downstream process, from lead scoring to sales follow-up, never sees them.
Body: Qualifying without forms means combining three things: knowing who is visiting, talking to them while they are on the page, and deciding automatically who deserves a sales conversation.

## SECTION 2 - Step by step
H2: Five steps.
1. **Write down your ICP** - Define the industries, company sizes, regions and needs of your best customers, and the behaviour that signals intent, such as repeat pricing-page visits.
2. **Identify visitors** - Use visitor identification and enrichment to match anonymous visits to companies, and where possible people, and to recognise them across sessions.
3. **Engage in real time** - Offer a conversation on high-intent pages. A grounded AI agent can answer questions from your approved content and capture budget, timeline and use case naturally, instead of through form fields.
4. **Score and route automatically** - Score each visitor against your ICP and use if/then rules to send qualified leads to the right rep or team, with the transcript attached.
5. **Book and follow up** - Let qualified visitors book a meeting in the conversation, and follow up automatically with the ones who leave without booking.

## SECTION 3 - Getting the rules right
H2: Practical tips.
- **Start with few rules** - Route by the two or three factors that matter most, such as region and company size, before adding more.
- **Keep context with the lead** - Reps respond better when the CRM record includes the conversation, pages viewed and enrichment data.
- **Hand off, don't guess** - When the agent can't answer from approved content, a human handoff protects trust and still captures the lead.
- **Review weekly** - Read a sample of conversations and handoffs, and close the knowledge gaps they reveal.

## FAQ
Q: Is identifying website visitors legal?
A: It depends on your jurisdiction and how it is done. You need a lawful basis, the right notices and any required consent. Thosjod's identification runs within a configurable, consent-aware framework designed to support those obligations.

Q: Do I still need forms?
A: Forms still work for visitors who prefer them. The point is not to depend on them, so the visitors who never fill one in are no longer invisible.

Q: What should an ICP include?
A: Firmographics such as industry, company size and region, plus the needs and behaviours that signal fit and intent.
