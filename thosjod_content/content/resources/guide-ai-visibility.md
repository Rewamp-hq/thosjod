## SECTION 1 - Why it matters
H2: Buyers now ask AI first.
Body: More buyers research by asking an AI assistant a question instead of searching and clicking through results. If the answer recommends a competitor and never mentions you, you lose the buyer before they ever reach your website, and you cannot see it happening in your analytics.
Body: Tracking AI visibility means asking the same questions your buyers ask, on the engines they use, on a regular schedule, and recording whether you appear.

## SECTION 2 - Step by step
H2: Six steps to a tracking programme.
1. **Write the prompts your buyers ask** - Start with 10 to 20 real questions across the funnel: category questions ("best tool for…"), comparison questions ("X vs Y"), and problem questions ("how do I…"). Use your sales team's call notes and your site search terms as sources.
2. **Pick the engines that matter** - ChatGPT and Gemini are a sensible starting pair. Add Perplexity, Claude, Copilot, Grok or Google's AI Overviews when your buyers use them.
3. **Name your competitors** - List the three to five brands that appear alongside you most often, so every answer is checked for them too.
4. **Check on a schedule** - AI answers change as models and the web change. Weekly checks show trends; daily checks catch fast shifts in competitive categories.
5. **Record more than yes or no** - Note whether you were mentioned, where in the answer, how you were described, which competitors appeared, and which sources the engine cited.
6. **Turn gaps into fixes** - When you are missing, look at what the answer cites and what your own pages lack, then fix the content, structure and third-party presence behind it.

## SECTION 3 - What to fix when you are missing
H2: Common causes and fixes.
- **Your pages don't answer the question directly** - Add a clear, quotable answer near the top of the relevant page, then the detail.
- **Engines can't tell what your company does** - Use one consistent definition of your product everywhere, and add Organization and product structured data.
- **Your FAQs aren't machine-readable** - Mark FAQ sections up with FAQPage structured data.
- **Competitors are cited from third-party sites** - Earn mentions on the review sites, directories and publications the engines already cite.
- **AI crawlers can't reach your content** - Check that robots.txt allows the AI crawlers you want to be cited by.

## FAQ
Q: How many prompts should I track?
A: Start with 10 to 20 that cover category, comparison and problem questions, then add more as you learn which ones drive decisions in your market.

Q: Why do AI answers change from one check to the next?
A: Models are updated, and engines with web search pull in new sources, so the same question can get a different answer over time. That is why tracking on a schedule matters more than a one-off check.
