## SECTION 1 - What are cookies
Cookies are small text files a website stores in your browser. Similar technologies, such as local storage and pixels, work in comparable ways; this policy covers them too.

## SECTION 2 - Cookies we use on thosjod.com
We group cookies by purpose. Non-essential cookies are only set after you accept them in our cookie banner, and you can change your choice at any time.
| Category | Purpose | Examples | Needs consent |
|---|---|---|---|
| Strictly necessary | Keep the site working and remember your cookie choice | Consent preference | No |
| Analytics | Understand how visitors use the site so we can improve it | Google Analytics 4, Microsoft Clarity (only if enabled) | Yes |
| Advertising | Measure B2B campaigns | LinkedIn Insight Tag (only if enabled) | Yes |
| Functional | Remember preferences such as the product-preview view | Local storage | No |

## SECTION 3 - The Thosjod widget on customers' websites
When a business installs Thosjod on its website, the widget uses session storage to keep a visitor's conversation and visit together. That business is responsible for the cookie notice and consent on its own site; please see its cookie policy.

## SECTION 4 - Managing cookies
You can accept or reject non-essential cookies in our banner, and change your choice later with the "Cookie settings" link in the site footer. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings; blocking strictly necessary cookies may affect how the site works.
