## SECTION 1 - The Problem

H2: The gap this closes
Body: Visitors bounce before finding what they need, and static, one-size-fits-all pages don't adapt to who's actually looking at them.

## SECTION 2 - The Solution

H2: How Thosjod solves it
Body: Thosjod reads visitor context the moment they land and tailors the conversation and content in real time, without a page reload.

## SECTION 3 - All 5 Features in This Pillar

| Feature (What)                            | How It Works                                                                                                                          | Why It Matters (Problem Solved)                                          |
| ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Hyper-personalized website experience** | AI agent embedded on the site reads visitor context (page, referrer, behavior) and tailors the conversation instantly, no page reload | Visitors bounce before finding what they need; no frictionless discovery |
| **Dynamic content serving**               | Agent swaps blocks of on-page content/messaging in real time based on visitor signals (industry, intent, stage)                       | Generic, one-size-fits-all content shown to every visitor                |
| **Show only most relevant content**       | Content-matching engine filters the knowledge base to surface only what's relevant to the visitor's stated need                       | Information overload / cluttered pages reduce conversion                 |
| **Guide visitor to next best step**       | Conversation flow logic recommends a specific next action (book demo, read case study, talk to sales) based on journey stage          | Visitors get stuck mid-journey and drop off                              |
| **Custom, on-brand AI agent**             | Agent is configured with your brand voice, tone, and approved messaging during onboarding                                             | Generic bots feel robotic and don't reflect brand voice                  |

## FAQ

Q: Does this require redesigning our website?
A: No - the agent and dynamic content layer overlay your existing site; no rebuild required.

Q: Can we control what the agent says?
A: Yes - Agent configuration (see Integration & Administration) lets you set tone, scope, and approved messaging.
