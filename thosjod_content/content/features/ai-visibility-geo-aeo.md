## SECTION 1 - The Problem

H2: The gap this closes
Body: Brands are invisible to buyers who now ask AI instead of Google, with no way to measure it - and most 'AI visibility' tools simulate answers rather than testing live ones.

## SECTION 2 - The Solution

H2: How Thosjod solves it
Body: Prompts run against live AI engines - including Gemini with Google Search grounding, not a simulation - tracking brand and competitor mentions, backed by a rule-based, explainable GEO/AEO audit that ties every lost point to a specific fixable issue.

## FAQ

Q: Is this a simulation of AI answers or real tracking?
A: Real - prompts run against live Gemini responses with Google Search grounding enabled, not cached or simulated output.

Q: What does the GEO/AEO audit actually check?
A: A rules engine checks structured data, content depth, and FAQ markup, among other factors, and ties every lost point to a specific, fixable issue.

Q: Which AI engines can visitors access directly?
A: ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, Perplexity, and Google AI, via one-click, page-aware prompts.
