## SECTION 1 - The Problem

H2: The gap this closes
Body: There's no clear line from website activity to revenue impact, and data is fragmented across disconnected tools with no single view of performance.

## SECTION 2 - The Solution

H2: How Thosjod solves it
Body: Every conversation and lead is tied to downstream CRM stage and revenue outcome, with funnel tracking, sentiment scoring, heat maps, and knowledge-gap insights in one dashboard.

## SECTION 3 - All 9 Features in This Pillar

| Feature (What)                                | How It Works                                                                                      | Why It Matters (Problem Solved)                                |
| --------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Revenue analysis & optimization**           | Ties each conversation and lead to downstream CRM stage/revenue outcome for attribution reporting | No clear line from website activity to revenue impact          |
| **Performance overview dashboard**            | Single dashboard aggregates engagement, leads, meetings, and revenue metrics                      | No single view of how the engine is performing                 |
| **Conversation quality & sentiment analysis** | NLP scoring model rates each conversation's tone, resolution, and visitor satisfaction            | No way to gauge conversation quality or customer sentiment     |
| **Conversion funnel tracking**                | Tracks visitor progression through defined funnel stages and flags drop-off points                | Blind spots in the funnel - no visibility into drop-off points |
| **Visitor geography & engagement sources**    | Logs visitor location and referral source alongside every session for segmentation                | No insight into where traffic and engagement come from         |
| **Knowledge gap insights**                    | Flags recurring questions the knowledge base answered poorly or not at all                        | Unknown gaps in content - questions the site can't answer well |
| **Heat map mapping**                          | Aggregates click/scroll/attention data across page sessions into a visual heat map                | No visibility into where visitors focus attention on a page    |
| **Analytics dashboard (general)**             | Central reporting layer underlying all the above metrics                                          | Fragmented data spread across disconnected tools               |
| **AI visibility score (baseline)**            | Aggregate score summarizing brand presence across tracked AI engines                              | No way to gauge visibility to AI-driven answer engines         |
