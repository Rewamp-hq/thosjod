## SECTION 1 - The Problem

H2: The gap this closes
Body: Reps aren't available 24/7, support teams are overloaded by repetitive questions, and generic AI risks hallucinating answers that damage trust.

## SECTION 2 - The Solution

H2: How Thosjod solves it
Body: A Sales Agent and Support Agent, each trained on your playbook and knowledge base, answer in text or voice, in the visitor's language, restricted to retrieval from approved content - with human handoff as the fallback, never a guess.

## SECTION 3 - All 8 Features in This Pillar

| Feature (What)                                     | How It Works                                                                                                   | Why It Matters (Problem Solved)                                   |
| -------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Sales Agent**                                    | A configured agent trained on your sales playbook handles discovery, qualification, and meeting booking        | Reps unavailable 24/7; slow response to inbound interest          |
| **Support Agent**                                  | Agent trained on your help docs/FAQ answers questions, escalates to a ticket + human handoff when needed       | Support teams overloaded by repetitive questions                  |
| **General AI conversational agent**                | Core LLM-based conversation engine underlying every agent type                                                 | No always-on assistance for visitors                              |
| **Voice-based conversational agent**               | Speech-to-text/text-to-speech layer lets visitors talk to the agent instead of typing                          | Visitors who prefer speaking over typing are underserved          |
| **Multilingual support**                           | Agent auto-detects visitor language and responds natively, translating knowledge base content on the fly       | Non-native-language visitors and international leads are excluded |
| **Visitors speak to widget in preferred language** | Same multilingual engine extended to the voice channel                                                         | Added friction from manual translation or language mismatch       |
| **No hallucination - grounded responses**          | Agent answers are restricted to retrieval from your approved knowledge base (RAG), with human handoff fallback | AI trust risk; wrong answers damage brand credibility             |
| **Optimize chat strategy**                         | A/B testing and analytics on conversation flows surface which scripts/prompts convert best                     | Static scripts that never improve conversion over time            |
