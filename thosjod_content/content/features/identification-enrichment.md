## SECTION 1 - The Problem

H2: The gap this closes
Body: Roughly 98% of website visitors leave without ever being identified, because identification has historically required a form.

## SECTION 2 - The Solution

H2: How Thosjod solves it
Body: Thosjod matches visitor signals against B2B identity graphs and enrichment databases in real time, then follows that identity across sessions, devices, and subdomains.

## SECTION 3 - All 6 Features in This Pillar

| Feature (What)                                   | How It Works                                                                                                    | Why It Matters (Problem Solved)                                              |
| ------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Identify anonymous website visitors**          | Matches visitor device/browser/IP signals against B2B identity graphs and enrichment databases in real time     | ~98% of visitors leave unseen and uncaptured                                 |
| **Capture real name, email & company info**      | Combines identity resolution with third-party enrichment providers to populate contact fields without a form    | No way to follow up with visitors who never submit a form                    |
| **Enrich data on the fly**                       | Appends firmographic/contact data to the visitor profile the moment they're identified, mid-session             | Incomplete lead data slows or blocks follow-up                               |
| **Purpose-built deanonymization of web traffic** | Dedicated identity-resolution pipeline runs continuously across all site traffic, not just chat participants    | Anonymous, high-intent visitors are invisible to sales                       |
| **Universal end-user identity layer**            | A shared identity token follows the visitor across sessions/devices/subdomains so they're recognized every time | Visitor identity is fragmented across sessions/devices/properties            |
| **SSO**                                          | Standard SAML/OAuth login integration for internal team members accessing the admin dashboard                   | Login friction and security risk for enterprise users accessing the platform |

## FAQ

Q: Is visitor identification legal / consent-compliant?
A: Identification runs within a configurable, consent-aware framework - see /responsible-ai and /security.

Q: What if a visitor can't be identified?
A: The agent still engages and qualifies them conversationally - identification enriches the record when a match is found, it isn't required for engagement to work.
