## Why the Problem Exists

- Most websites and their tooling (forms, scripted chatbots, standalone CRMs) were built for a search-engine-and-form era, where the only visitors who could be "captured" were the ones who self-identified.
- Visitor identification technology (deanonymization, enrichment) exists today but is usually sold as a narrow, standalone tool (see: Warmly) rather than fused with the conversational layer that acts on it.
- The shift of buyer research toward AI answer engines (ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity) is recent enough that no tool in the competitive set has built visibility tracking for it - brands have no equivalent of "Google Search Console" for AI answers.

## Who It Solves It For

Any organization with **meaningful website traffic and a conversion rate lower than that traffic justifies** - see the 14 ICPs in `04b-icp-summary.md`. The pattern repeats across very different traffic types: PLG self-serve research (SaaS), high-stakes low-frequency decisions (BFSI, healthcare, legal), seasonal surges (universities, travel), and support-heavy volume (telecom, government).

## How Thosjod Solves It

Through 9 connected capability pillars (not 9 separate tools): a visitor is identified and enriched the moment they land (Pillar 2), engaged through a personalized, grounded conversation (Pillars 1 & 4), qualified and routed automatically (Pillar 3), followed up with via automated playbooks (Pillar 5), synced into existing CRM/ops tools (Pillar 6), measured end-to-end (Pillar 7), and - uniquely - tracked and optimized for visibility in AI answer engines (Pillar 8), with all of it rolling up into the outcome metrics leadership actually tracks (Pillar 9).

## What Makes the Workflow Different

Competitors in the dataset each solve one stage of the journey (identification _or_ conversation _or_ outbound _or_ AI visibility). Thosjod's differentiation is **structural, not just feature-count**: the same visitor session carries through identification → conversation → qualification → routing → analytics as one continuous record, rather than requiring separate tools to be stitched together with brittle integrations.

## Business Outcomes It's Designed to Influence

Directly mapped to Pillar 9 (Outcome Metrics): inbound meeting volume, direct customer acquisition, chat-to-lead conversion lift, consultation close rate, customer churn rate, engagement/CTA click rate, and buying-experience quality.
