## Product

**Q: What makes Thosjod different from a chatbot?**
A: Chatbots answer questions from visitors who choose to open the widget. Thosjod also identifies and engages the visitors who never open it at all.

**Q: Is this one product or several integrated tools?**
A: One product. Identification, conversation, qualification, automation, analytics, and AI-visibility tracking share the same visitor record and admin configuration.

**Q: Does Thosjod replace our CRM?**
A: No - it syncs into your existing CRM (HubSpot, Salesforce) rather than replacing it.

**Q: Can we use only some layers (e.g., just Support, not Sales)?**
A: Yes - Agent configuration lets you enable and tune which agents and layers are active.

## AI / Accuracy

**Q: Can the AI agent give wrong answers?**
A: Every response is grounded in your approved knowledge base, with human handoff instead of a guess.

**Q: What happens when the agent doesn't know the answer?**
A: It hands off to a human or creates a support ticket rather than guessing.

**Q: Is AI visibility tracking a simulation or real?**
A: Real - prompts run against live Gemini responses with Google Search grounding enabled, not cached or simulated output.

## GEO / AEO

**Q: What is AI Visibility / GEO/AEO?**
A: The practice of tracking and improving how your brand is represented in AI-generated answers, the way SEO tracks search-engine visibility.

**Q: What does the GEO/AEO audit check?**
A: Structured data, content depth, FAQ markup, and more - every lost point tied to a specific fixable issue.

**Q: Which AI engines can visitors access directly?**
A: ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, Perplexity, and Google AI.
