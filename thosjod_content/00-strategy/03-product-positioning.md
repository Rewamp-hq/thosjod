# OUTPUT 04 - Product Positioning

## Positioning Statement (master)

**For** organizations with meaningful website traffic and a conversion rate that doesn't match it, **Thosjod is** an AI Website Discovery & Lead Engine **that** identifies, engages, and qualifies every visitor in real time - form or no form - and natively tracks how the brand shows up across AI answer engines, **unlike** website chatbots, visitor-ID tools, or AI SDR platforms that each solve only one stage of this problem, **because** Thosjod carries one visitor session continuously through identification, conversation, qualification, routing, and analytics, with AI-visibility tracking (GEO/AEO) built in from day one rather than bolted on.

## Differentiation Pillars (USPs)

1. **Captures the visitors every form-based tool misses.** Deanonymization + enrichment identify real name, email, and company for visitors who never fill out a form - the majority of traffic on most sites.
2. **Grounded, not guessing.** Every AI response is restricted to the approved knowledge base, with human handoff instead of hallucination - a specific, named guardrail, not a generic "safe AI" claim.
3. **AI visibility, built into the lead engine.** Unlike website chatbots, visitor-ID tools and AI SDR platforms, Thosjod tracks AI visibility natively - in the same engine that converts your visitors. None of the 40 chatbot, visitor-ID, SDR and conversational-AI competitors analyzed (Output 13) show AI-visibility tracking. _Updated 2026-09-26:_ dedicated AI-visibility/GEO platforms do exist (Profound, Otterly.AI, Peec AI, Scrunch, AthenaHQ, Ahrefs Brand Radar, Semrush - see `content/comparisons/research/ai-visibility-tools.csv`). Several of them go deeper on AI-search analytics, but none of the pages reviewed describe identifying or converting website visitors. **Never claim "the only product that tracks AI visibility."** The defensible claim is the combination: visitor conversion plus AI visibility in one engine.
4. **One continuous visitor record, not a stitched-together stack.** Identification, conversation, qualification, and analytics share one session - most competitors solve one stage and require integration glue for the rest.
5. **No-code, fast time-to-value.** Configure and launch without an engineering sprint (Pillar 6).

## What Thosjod Is Not Positioned As

- Not a general-purpose customer-support helpdesk (like Ada or Kommunicate) - support is one agent type among several, not the core identity.
- Not a pure visitor-identification data vendor (like Warmly) - identification feeds a conversational and qualification workflow, not a standalone alert feed.
- Not an outbound/SDR automation tool (like Artisan or 11x) - outbound follow-up is a downstream capability of an inbound-first engine, not the primary use case.
- Not a general enterprise conversational-AI platform (like Yellow.ai or Haptik) - Thosjod is GTM/lead-engine-specific, not a horizontal CX automation suite.
