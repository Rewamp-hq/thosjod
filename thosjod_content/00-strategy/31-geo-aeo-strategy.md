## Structural Practices Applied Site-Wide

- **Entity-first definitions** - every feature/pillar page opens its Problem/Solution sections with a direct, quotable definition sentence rather than burying it in narrative.
- **Consistent terminology** - "AI Website Discovery & Lead Engine" used identically across Homepage, Platform, and all comparison pages (not varied for "freshness," which fragments how AI engines associate the term with Thosjod).
- **FAQ schema everywhere** - every page with an FAQ section should render `FAQPage` structured data (dev requirement, not just content).
- **Comparison pages as citable answers** - the `/compare/*` pages are written in the exact Q&A pattern AI engines favor when asked "how does X compare to Y" - this is a deliberate GEO play, not incidental.
